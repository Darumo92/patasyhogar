## Proposed final outline

Open directly with the purpose of temporary first aid, contacting veterinary care and urgent action for severe signs. Avoid narrative urgency or invented rescues.

1. `¿Qué hacer primero ante una emergencia con tu perro?` Safe surroundings, call the clinic, explain what happened and organize transport [F1–F3].
2. `Cómo acercarte y trasladar a un perro herido` Bite risk, cautious handling, minimum movement after trauma; no improvised muzzle tutorial [F1–F2].
3. `Heridas, hemorragias y mordeduras` Clean pad and steady pressure, add layers; urgent veterinary assessment for deep/persistent injuries [F1, F3].
4. `¿Qué hacer si tu perro tiene una convulsión?` Clear nearby hazards, do not restrain or put hands in the mouth; contact care, explain duration/repetition [F1, F3].
5. `Atragantamiento, dificultad para respirar o pérdida de consciencia` Recognize urgency and seek professional instructions; no blind mouth sweep, compression recipe or CPR tutorial [F1, F7].
